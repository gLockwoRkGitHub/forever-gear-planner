Where an item may come from.

My professions

Unlocks bind-on-pickup crafts and gear that needs the profession to equip. Tradeable crafts always show.

Extras

Only upgrades: hide items that are not better than what you wear. Random-enchant items are scored on base stats only.

Stat weights
Score = sum of stat × weight. Weapons add DPS × weapon-DPS weight plus a bonus per 0.1 s of speed above 3.0. Starting values are estimates, not simulated.

Level and equipped gear come from the armory (or Blizzard's API) and refresh every hour; the armory updates after you log out. Setting a level by hand lasts until the armory reports a new level. Each character keeps its own level, filters, professions and weights. Shaman: two-handed axes, maces and staves, mail from level 40. Druid: staves, two-handed maces and polearms from level 20, cloth and leather only; in Forever, feral attacks scale with weapon damage. Items show up from the level you can realistically get them: the item's required level; for quest rewards the quest's minimum level, but no earlier than 3 levels below the quest's level.

Crafted items that bind on pickup only count for the professions ticked under "My professions"; crafted items that bind on equip always count, since you can buy them or have them made. Items that need a profession to equip follow the same list.

Item stats come from Wowhead's WoW Forever tooltips; drop, quest and vendor sources from QuestieDB's Forever database; crafting from the game data. Items Blizzard still hides (not yet discovered) are missing. Special equip effects are listed but not scored.

Hey Matze, here are some addon ideas

This planner knows the item database. The game knows what we actually wear, loot and finish. A small addon of our own could connect the two. Here is what it could do. Tell Pasco what you would use most.

How game and page would talk

Addons can't go online. The addon shows a short text you copy into this page, or the page gives you a text to paste into the addon. On a PC the page can also load the addon's saved file. Inside a group, our two addons can message each other directly.

From the game to the page

Import your gear

Done without an addon: the page now reads level and equipped items from the armory every hour and shows real upgrades over what you wear.

Hide finished quests

The game knows every quest you completed. Their rewards drop out of the lists, and a quest checklist fills itself. Fits the quest-sync addon idea.

Fill the data gaps

The game shows stats for items Wowhead hasn't revealed yet. The addon notes what we see, with boss or quest, and hands it to the page.

From the page into the game

Upgrade line in tooltips

Every item tooltip shows what it is worth for each of us, like Shaman +4.2 · Druid +6.1. Works for loot, vendors, the auction house and quest rewards.

Quest reward pick

When a quest offers a choice, the addon marks the best reward for your character.

Dungeon cheat sheet

When you enter a dungeon, a small window lists which boss drops an upgrade for whom, taken from the dungeon planner.

Between the two of us

Loot call

In a group, both addons share gear and weights. When something drops, both of us see who gains more, like Druid +8.0 · Shaman +1.5.

Suggested order
